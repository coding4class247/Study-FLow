Observation 1:
Observation Statement:
People want an easy to read fully put together hub for their assignments and projects. Their classes rely on multiple different software platforms therefore making it harder to study and know exactly what is needed, and where to apply effort. 
Supporting Data:
This is supported by the majority of peoples' way that they decide which assignments to tackle. Most responders answered that they just tackle whichever due date is the soonest and go from there in a chronological order for the next assignment due.  
Team Action (if any):
The team can act upon this data in two ways, first we can make our app a true hub for all assignments, projects, quizzes, and exams and create a calendar suite within the app to keep track of all stats regarding assignments. Finally we can create a list suite that just shows all assignments in chronological order and can be structured like a game with small rewards like profile pictures, different color schemes for the app etc allowing for a gamification of academics leading to students having an easier time committing effort. 
Observation 2:
Observation Statement:
 A major frustration for students is the amount of manual work required to organize assignments. Many students appear interested in a feature that could automatically collect important assignment information for them instead of requiring them to enter every deadline 
Supporting Data:
Out of the 16 responses shown , 9 students said they were "extremely interested” in a feature that could instantly add assignment information while another 4 said somewhat interested. Students also mentioned how frustrating it was to keep having to go back to brightspace and remembering deadlines 
Team Action (if any):
We could prioritize an automatic assignment-import feature.
Observation 3:
Observation Statement:
The base of this survey consists primarily of senior and junior college students. Surprisingly many of them opt for pen and paper means of keeping track of assignments.
Supporting Data:
When asked what grade level the respondees were, the vast majority said junior and senior college students. When asked what they currently used, many opted to use paper means of tracking over digital action.
Team Action (if any):
Consider a means of turning physical schedules digital either by adding camera functionality or supporting pdf uploads so that third party apps can convert instead.
Observation 4:
Observation Statement:
Based on the survey, many people study for whatever they have coming up sooner rather than by the assumed difficulty or time needed on one particular assignment or exam.
Supporting Data:
Out of the 16 people who took the survey, 12/16 people chose “I tackle whatever is due soonest” for the question of deciding what to study for first when there are multiple competing deadlines. 
Team Action (if any):
We could make a section that lists upcoming assignments, and their weight for the class it is in.
Observation 5:
Observation Statement:
People expressed strong interest in a feature that converts a photo of their syllabus into a living schedule.
Supporting Data:
When asked about this feature, many of the responses had chosen “extremely interesting, It would save so much time”.
Team Action (if any):
There is already a feature in many applications that, like in Brightspace, allow you to link your living digital schedule to calendars of your choice (phone, google, laptop, wherever), via a link. We could do something similar and make it more noticeable for the user.
