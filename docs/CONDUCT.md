305Soft Code of Conduct & Team Operating Policies
1. Community Code of Conduct.
Community Standards
To create a positive and productive environment, team members and contributors are expected to:
* Use welcoming and inclusive language: Ensure communication is approachable and respectful.
* Respect diverse perspectives: Value differing viewpoints, backgrounds, and experiences.
* Accept constructive feedback: Gracefully accept and act on "cold" (constructive) feedback.
* Prioritize the team: Align individual effort with what is best for the overall project and community.
* Practice empathy: Demonstrate empathy and support toward fellow team members.
Unacceptable Behavior
The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:
* Publishing private information (such as physical or email addresses) without explicit consent
* Any conduct reasonably deemed inappropriate in a professional or academic setting
2. Team Operating Policies
Technology Policy
* No Distracted Technology: To maintain fully engaged, present collaboration, all phones and non-essential electronic devices must be put away during team meetings.
Meeting Logistics, Attendance & Promptness
* Scheduled Block: Primary team meetings take place on Mondays from 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Virtual meetings will be scheduled as needed.
* Attendance Expectations: In-person attendance is strongly expected.
* Handling Absences:
   * If you have a legitimate conflict preventing in-person attendance, you may attend virtually or work on the project asynchronously.
   * Make-Up Requirement: Any member working asynchronously must submit an update.txt file outlining their progress and accomplishments during that session to keep the team informed.
* Punctuality: Punctuality is required. Habitual lateness undermines trust and team reliability. Repeated tardiness will be taken seriously and escalated directly to the professor.
Roles & Responsibilities
* Agenda & Notes Master (Samuel): Responsible for drafting meeting agendas, taking detailed meeting minutes, and promptly distributing notes to the group.
Decision-Making Process
* Majority Vote: All operational and project-related decisions are determined by a simple majority vote.
* Tie-Breaker: In the event of a tie, the Scrum Master holds the deciding vote to prevent project gridlock.
Task Assignments & Accountability
* Elective Task Claiming: Assignments are elective. After the group identifies required deliverables, members may volunteer for tasks aligned with their strengths and growth goals.
* Resolving Task Conflicts: If multiple members request the same task or disagree on execution strategies:
   1. Interested parties draft a brief proposal.
   2. The team discusses, mediates, and votes on the best approach.
* Accountability: Full participation is strictly enforced. While occasional setbacks occur, flagrant or repeated failure to complete assigned tasks will trigger a mandatory group intervention. Unresolved issues will be escalated to the professor.
Communication & Feedback
* Active Listening: Members must respect airtime and actively listen to all contributions.
* Structured Feedback: Meetings will regularly incorporate a Warm & Cold Feedback framework:
   * Warm Feedback: Positive reinforcement and recognition of good work.
   * Cold Feedback: Constructive, actionable critique aimed at continuous improvement.
3. Enforcement & Escalation Policy
Project maintainers and leadership are responsible for clarifying standards and maintaining a productive team dynamic. Violations of the Code of Conduct or Team Operating Policies will be handled via a two-step escalation model:
1. First Offense: An internal verbal warning and team mediation session.
2. Repeated or Flagrant Offenses: Direct escalation to the professor to review policy non-compliance and determine further academic consequences.
